Grocery Matcher

How two grocery catalogs get reconciled into one matched row per product — cheap, wide retrieval first; one expensive, structured judgment call last.

  • 166 MBStore A CSV
  • 67 MBStore B CSV
  • 3retrieval paths

End-to-end flow

Store B is embedded and indexed once, up front. Store A never gets its own index — it streams through in batches, querying Store B's index three different ways before a single GPT call adjudicates the merged candidates.

The adjudication gate

GPT's decision field is advisory, not final. The pipeline independently checks four conditions on the structured response — any single failure rejects the match, regardless of what the model claimed.
